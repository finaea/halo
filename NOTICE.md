## Visual design — based on Rainformer (CC BY-NC 3.0)

Halo's widget design is based on the **Rainformer 3.1 HWiNFO Edition** Rainmeter skin by
**Pul53dr1v3r**, published on DeviantArt under
**[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/)**:

> Rainformer 3.1 HWiNFO Edition — Pul53dr1v3r
> https://www.deviantart.com/pul53dr1v3r/art/Rainformer-3-1-HWiNFO-Edition-Rainmeter-789616481

What is based on it: the colour palette (taken from the skin's `@Resources\Variables.inc`), the
panel sizes and row layout (transcribed into `tools/extracted/*.json`), and the arrangement and
wording of the meters. No Rainmeter code is included — Halo is a new native app written from
scratch — but the *design* is the original author's work, and this notice is the attribution that
licence asks for.

Halo's **Rainformer** skin carries that design. *Rainformer Light* keeps the original palette;
*Rainformer Dark* adapts the factory dark palette for clearer text. Any third-party palettes are
credited in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

**Noncommercial.** CC BY-NC 3.0 does not allow commercial use of the licensed work or of works
based on it. Halo's own code is under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (`LICENSE`), which is also
noncommercial, so the two point the same way: Halo may be used, forked, modified and shared, but
not sold, included in something that is sold, or shipped as part of a commercial product. A
GitHub issue can be opened to discuss commercial use of Halo's code; the Rainformer design would

Three icon fonts originally came from the Rainformer skin folder. Only one is still shipped

The **Azur Archive** skin is an unofficial fan work. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by NEXON Games, Yostar, Manjuu or Yongshi, and nothing in it is meant to look official.

It uses character art and a few lines of in-game text from two games, kept in one folder,
`assets/skins/azur-archive/game-art/`:

- **Blue Archive** — © NEXON Games & Yostar. Official channel:
  <https://www.youtube.com/@BlueArchive_Global>
- **Azur Lane** — © Manjuu, Yongshi & Yostar (Manjuu only).

Every file is listed in that folder's `CREDITS.md` with its source and owner. The art and game
text are used **without a licence** and stay their owners' property; they are **not** covered by
Halo's PolyForm Noncommercial licence, which applies to Halo's code only. Halo is noncommercial,
has no paid features and asks for no donations.

**Removed on request.** A rights holder can open a GitHub issue naming the files. Removal follows
[docs/game-art-removal.md](docs/game-art-removal.md). The skin works without the art, which can also
be hidden with the **Game art** option in Settings.

The skin's layout and drawn elements (cards, halos, overlays, Manjuu's sign) were made for Halo, and
its colours were chosen to echo the two games' interfaces; no interface graphics from either game
are included. Its fonts are Barlow Condensed, M PLUS Rounded 1c and Oxanium, all under the SIL Open Font
License — see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
