# Azur Archive — game art credits

The bundled game art and text in this folder stay their owners' property and are **not** covered
by Halo's PolyForm Noncommercial licence. Halo is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by NEXON Games, Yostar, Manjuu or Yongshi. The art and game text are used without a
licence and removed on request. A rights holder can open a GitHub issue naming the files;
[docs/game-art-removal.md](../../../../docs/game-art-removal.md) describes the removal steps.

- **Blue Archive** © NEXON Games & Yostar. <https://www.youtube.com/@BlueArchive_Global>
- **Azur Lane** © Manjuu, Yongshi & Yostar.

The images come from fan wikis; no game client was extracted.
Each was decoded from the wiki's WebP delivery, cropped (face crop or bust) and saved as PNG.
All-ages art only; no Bunny or Swimsuit variants.

Crops: card faces are 256 × 256 px with the face about two thirds of the frame (the 28-unit card
slot is 48 px at 1.7× and 196 px at 2 × 3.5×), companion busts 512 × 512 px. Naming follows the
slot chain `<character>-<mood>.png` → `<character>.png` + vector overlay → no character.

## Generated previews

`previews/<preset>.png` are Settings-gallery pictures rendered by `build.ps1` (`Halo.Widgets.exe
--render <assets> --previews`) from the files above. They contain the art, so they live in this
folder and go with it; the gallery falls back to the art-free set in `..\previews\` when they are
absent.
